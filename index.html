02 — FLAGSHIP

LumiR: assistive navigation robot

An AI-integrated mobile robot that helps people with visual impairments move safely and independently. LumiR combines 240° LiDAR, computer vision and wireless haptic feedback — guidance reaches the user through a vibrating glove and bone-conduction audio, leaving their own senses free.

04 — PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Dr. Son Tien Bui

MEMBER, IEEE · LECTURER, HAUI

Son Tien Bui (Member, IEEE) received his Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Mechanical Engineering from Hanoi University of Science and Technology, Vietnam, in 2009 and 2012 respectively. He completed his Ph.D. in Robotics at the Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST), Japan, in 2023, under the Soft Haptics Lab.

He has managed Fablab HaUI — an open laboratory for student research — since 2016, and leads Soft Robotics Lab as a research initiative focused on bio-inspired robotics, soft sensing and human-centered assistive systems.
